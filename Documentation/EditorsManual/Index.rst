**How to use this document:**

This document provides step-by-step instructions for editors using the OAuth2 extension.

----

**Logging In**

1. Go to your TYPO3 backend login page
2. Click on your OAuth2 provider (e.g., "Login with GitLab")
3. Authenticate with your provider
4. You will be automatically redirected back to TYPO3

----

**First-Time Login**

When logging in for the first time:

1. Your account will be automatically created
2. You will be assigned to appropriate backend groups
3. Your permissions will be set based on your provider settings

----

**Accessing the Backend**

After successful login:

1. You will see the standard TYPO3 backend interface
2. Your access rights will be based on your assigned groups
3. You can use all TYPO3 features according to your permissions

----

**Common Tasks**

* **Logging In**: Use your OAuth2 provider credentials
* **Logging Out**: Use the standard TYPO3 logout button
* **Switching Users**: Log out and log in with different credentials
* **Accessing Help**: Use TYPO3's built-in help system

----

**Troubleshooting**

If you encounter problems:

* **Cannot Log In**:
  * Check your provider credentials
  * Ensure you have the correct permissions
  * Contact your administrator

* **Missing Permissions**:
  * Verify your provider account settings
  * Contact your administrator for access

* **Login Errors**:
  * Try logging out and back in
  * Clear your browser cache
  * Contact support if issues persist

----

**Security Best Practices**

* Never share your provider credentials
* Use secure networks for login
* Log out when finished
* Report suspicious activity
* Keep your provider account secure

----

**Getting Help**

If you need assistance:

1. Check the TYPO3 documentation
2. Contact your administrator
3. Review provider-specific help
4. Check the extension's support channels

----

**Next Steps**

After familiarizing yourself with the basics:

* Review TYPO3's general documentation
